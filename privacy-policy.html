Privacy Policy

Welcome to SpeakSmart: Teleprompter & AI Speech Coach ("SpeakSmart", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by Factictionary. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles and practices regarding the collection, storage, use, and protection of information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Our Core Privacy Commitment: SpeakSmart is engineered with a privacy-first, on-device architecture. Your camera video recordings, audio streams, voice transcriptions, and written scripts are processed 100% locally on your device. We do not transmit, record, listen to, or store your voice, speech transcripts, or camera video on remote cloud servers.

🔒 100% Offline AI Speech Processing

Real-time speech-to-text transcription, filler word detection, and pace analysis run exclusively on your phone's processor.

📹 Local Video & Script Storage

Prompter scripts and recorded videos remain in your device's internal storage or photo gallery and are never uploaded to our servers.

🚫 Zero Voice Data Selling

We do not monetize, sell, license, or share your personal audio or video data with third parties or AI training farms.

1. Device Permissions & How We Use Them

To provide our Teleprompter and Speech Coaching features, SpeakSmart requests the following runtime permissions on your device:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used to detect your spoken words in real time for automatic teleprompter voice scrolling and speech coaching analytics (filler word counts, pace gauge, pauses). Audio is processed entirely in device RAM in real time and is discarded immediately after analysis.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used exclusively within the Teleprompter Studio to display camera preview and record video presentations while scrolling your script. Video frames are rendered and saved directly to your local device.
  • System Alert Window / Overlay (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Required on Android to render the Floating Prompter over other apps (e.g., Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram). No screen recording or background surveillance occurs.
  • Storage / Media: Used to import custom script files (.txt, .doc) from your device and export practice reports (.pdf) or recorded videos to your gallery.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely for optional local daily practice reminders configured by you.

2. Information We Collect

A. Data You Create Locally (Never Transmitted to Us)

  • Teleprompter Scripts: Text scripts, speech notes, and formatting settings saved in local SQLite database.
  • Recorded Videos & Audio: Media recorded in Teleprompter Studio saved to your local gallery.
  • Speech Analytics & History: Session scores, filler word statistics, streaks, and achievements saved locally.

B. Optional Account Data (Firebase Authentication)

If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive basic authentication identifiers through Google Firebase Authentication:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your profile picture URL.
  • A unique user authentication identifier (UID).

This information is used strictly to maintain your account identity and verify active subscription and lifetime purchases across devices.

C. Purchase & In-App Billing Data

In-app purchases (VIP Lifetime, Pro Subscriptions, Modular Passes) are handled directly by the Google Play Store Billing API. We do not receive, store, or process your credit card numbers, banking details, or billing addresses.

D. Anonymous Diagnostics & Advertising

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): For users on the free tier, Google AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers and diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies to display non-personalized or personalized ads. Users who purchase "Remove Ads", "VIP Lifetime", or "Pro Subscriptions" will experience zero advertising.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only integrate with trusted industry-standard SDKs for app infrastructure:

  • Google Play Services & In-App Purchase API: To process secure transactions.
  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): To manage secure user authentication.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): To serve banner and interstitial ads on the free tier.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your speech data, scripts, and practice history reside in your device's local database, you have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Clear All Data in App: You can wipe all local sessions, history, and scripts anytime via Settings → Clear All Data.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the application completely deletes all locally cached scripts and session databases.
  • Account Deletion: You may request full deletion of your Firebase authentication profile by emailing factictionary@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

SpeakSmart is designed for general audiences and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at factictionary@gmail.com and we will immediately remove such information.

6. Security of Your Information

We employ strict on-device data processing models, sandboxed local storage, and secure HTTPS/TLS encryption for authentication endpoints to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
